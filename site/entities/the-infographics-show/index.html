    The Infographics Show¶

    Overview¶

    The Infographics Show is a major YouTube educational channel with 15.5 million subscribers that produces explainer content on complex topics including economics, history, science, and current events. The channel is known for its visual approach to explaining complex concepts and has significant public engagement with its content.

    Channel Details¶

    • YouTube Subscribers: 15.5M
    • Content Type: Educational infographics and explainers
    • Narrator: Josh Risser
    • Format: Auto-dubbed for multiple languages
    • Social Media: Active on TikTok, Facebook, and Discord
    • Production: Professional video production with visual explanations
