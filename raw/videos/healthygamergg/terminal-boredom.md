## Key Concepts Covered

### Terminal Boredom
- Endstage manifestation of screen addiction
- People so intensely bored they can't do anything
- Progression through multiple stages

### Dopamine Tolerance Mechanism
- Receptor downregulation to protect from overstimulation
- Progression: 3 receptors → 2 receptors → 1 receptor
- Need higher stimulation (3x → 6x → 9x) for same response
- Eventually have very few receptors

### Executive Function Rust
- Body rusts if you don't use it, not wears out
- Screens do all the planning for you
- Example: Morrowind complex directions vs modern objective markers
- People can't break abstract tasks into pieces

### Impulse Control Atrophy
- Normal frontal lobe controls impulses
- Technology activates impulses instead of requiring control
- Autoplay, skip credits, skip intros - no restraint needed
- Platforms fight war to make you give into impulses

### Multi-circuit Evolution
- Singleplayer RPGs → MMOs → Progression → Agency → Mastery → Community → Identity
- Each addition activates more brain circuits
- Platforms compete by activating more circuits

### Clinical Manifestations
- Lack of willpower
- Don't know what to want in life
- Not motivated enough
- Get bored easily
- Can't focus
- Social difficulties
- "Everyone has ADHD now"
