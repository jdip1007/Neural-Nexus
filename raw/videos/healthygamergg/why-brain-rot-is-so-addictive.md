## Key Concepts Covered

### Dopamine Lemon Metaphor
- Brain starts with a "full lemon" of dopamine daily
- Hard squeezes (video games, scrolling) drain juice fast
- Gentle squeezes (work, chores) use juice efficiently
- Consequence: Hard squeezes early leave no motivation for gentle tasks

### Artificial Brain Circuit Activation
- Content artificially activates survival circuits (orgasm, hunger, social)
- Provides calories without nutrition → dopamine "obesity"
- Platform dynamics: Darwinian selection ensures most addictive content rises

### Rage Bait
- Spikes heart rate/BP (like exercise) without movement
- Causes cardiovascular damage
- Anger circuits hijacked without real threat

### Parasocial Relationships
- Activates social bonding circuits with fake relationships
- OnlyFans example: Transactional intimacy illusion
- Real oxytocin vs fake oxytocin problem

### Inflammation Cycle
- High screen time → elevated IL-6, leptin, insulin
- Inflammation → poor sleep → low willpower → can't resist brain rot → stay up late → worse sleep → repeat

### Health Consequences
- Cardiovascular: Blood clots & strokes in 20-somethings
- Metabolic: Insulin resistance, leptin dysregulation
- Immune: Elevated IL-6
- Atopic disease: Gaming/mobile phones worsen eczema, asthma

### Solutions
1. Identify the need: What do I get from this?
2. Broccoli Effect: Eating broccoli reduces candy cravings
3. Physiological interventions: Diet (2+ fruits/vegetables), exercise (30 min walk), sleep (7+ hours, Yoga Nidra)
