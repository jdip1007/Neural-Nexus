[00:00] How to Prepare for the Next Recession

[00:15] Economic cycles are inevitable. Recessions happen, but being prepared can make all the difference between weathering the storm and being devastated by it.

[00:30] The first step is building an emergency fund. Aim for 3-6 months of living expenses in a safe, liquid account. This isn't for investing - it's for covering essential expenses if you lose your income.

[00:45] Next, eliminate high-interest debt. Credit card debt and personal loans can become crushing during economic downturns. Focus on paying these down aggressively while times are good.

[01:00] Diversify your income. Relying on a single paycheck is risky. Develop side hustles, build skills that are in demand, and consider passive income streams that can survive economic uncertainty.

[01:15] Review your investments. During recessions, quality companies with strong balance sheets tend to recover faster. Avoid speculative assets that could disappear entirely.

[01:30] Maintain your skills. Your ability to earn income is your most valuable asset. Continuously upgrade your skills and stay adaptable to changing market demands.

[01:45] Don't panic sell. Market timing is nearly impossible. History shows that markets recover, but panic selling locks in losses and prevents you from benefiting from the eventual rebound.
