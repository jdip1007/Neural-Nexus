# Findings

This section contains processed insights and research findings derived from analysis of sources.

## About Findings

Findings pages represent the **output of research and analysis** — novel insights, patterns discovered, or conclusions reached through synthesis of multiple sources.

### When to Create a Finding

- When processing a source reveals a **novel insight** not captured elsewhere
- When cross-referencing multiple sources leads to a **new understanding**
- When data analysis uncovers **patterns or trends** worth documenting
- When a hypothesis is **supported or refuted** by evidence

### Structure

A finding page should include:
- **Question**: What was investigated?
- **Evidence**: What data or sources support this?
- **Analysis**: How was the evidence evaluated?
- **Conclusion**: What was learned?
- **Limitations**: What caveats apply?
- **Sources**: What materials were analyzed?
