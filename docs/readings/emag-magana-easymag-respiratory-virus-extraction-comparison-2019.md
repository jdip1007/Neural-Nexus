# eMAG vs MagNA PURE 96 vs easyMAG: Respiratory Virus Extraction Platform Comparison

**Source:** Hindiyeh M, Mor O, Pando R, et al. (2019). Comparison of the new fully automated extraction platform eMAG to the MagNA PURE 96 and the well-established easyMAG for detection of common human respiratory viruses. PLoS ONE, 14(2): e0211079. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0211079

**Study Site:** Central Virology Laboratory, Ministry of Health, Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel

**Sample Size:** 262 archived respiratory samples + quantitated virus controls

---

## TL;DR

All three nucleic acid extraction platforms (eMAG, MagNA PURE 96, easyMAG) demonstrated comparable analytical and clinical performance for detection of common respiratory viruses. Sensitivity >97% and specificity >98% across all platforms. Choice should be based on laboratory volume and workflow requirements rather than performance differences.

---

## Study Design

### Platforms Compared

| Platform | Manufacturer | Type | Capacity | Extraction Time | Technology |
|----------|-------------|------|----------|-----------------|------------|
| **eMAG** | bioMérieux (France) | Fully automated | 48 samples | ~90 min | Silica-based (BOOM technology) |
| **MagNA PURE 96** | Roche (Germany) | Fully automated | 96 samples | ~90 min | Magnetic bead technology |
| **easyMAG** | bioMérieux (France) | Semi-automated | 24 samples | ~60 min | Silica-based (BOOM technology) |

### Sample Types Tested

- **Bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL):** N = 8
- **Tracheal aspirations:** N = 32
- **Nasopharyngeal swabs:** N = 403
- **Total archived samples:** N = 262 (distributed across virus types)

### Viruses Targeted

1. **Influenza A** (N = 47 positive samples) - including H1N1pdm (N = 22)
2. **Influenza B** (N = 21)
3. **Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)** (N = 21)
4. **Human Metapneumovirus (hMPV)** (N = 23)
5. **Parainfluenza-3** (N = 20)
6. **Adenovirus** (N = 23)
7. **Negative controls** (N = 181)

### Methodology

^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

- Each sample divided into three tubes (500 μL each)
- Extracted simultaneously by all three platforms
- Elution volumes: 55 μL (easyMAG, eMAG) or 50 μL (MagNA PURE 96)
- Multiplex qPCR detection with in-house assays
- Positive threshold: Ct < 40
- Negative threshold: Ct ≥ 40 with RNase P internal control positive (Ct < 35)
- Invalid: No signal + negative internal control

### Limit of Detection (LOD) Testing

- Serial decimal dilutions (10⁻¹ to 10⁻⁸) of quantitated virus stocks
- WHO stocks: Influenza A/H3N2, Influenza A/H1N1pdm, Influenza B
- ATCC stocks: RSV, Adenovirus
- Clinical samples: hMPV, Parainfluenza-3
- LOD defined: ≥2/3 replicates positive

### Inter-platform Agreement

- **Kappa coefficient:** >0.973 (excellent agreement between eMAG and both comparison platforms)
- **Sample type agreement:** >97% across BAL, tracheal aspirations, and nasopharyngeal swabs
- **Ct value SD:** <1.0 across all platforms (excellent correlation)
- **Bland-Altman analysis:** >98% of samples within 2 SD boundaries for both comparisons

| Virus | easyMAG | eMAG | MagNA PURE 96 | Stock Source |

| **Influenza A** | 10⁻² | 10⁻³ | 10⁻³ | WHO |

| **H1N1pdm** | 10⁻⁴ | 10⁻⁴ | 10⁻⁴ | WHO |

**Key Observation:** LOD differences were within one dilution factor for all viruses. These differences are considered negligible as high dilution samples have very low virus concentrations and are mostly undetected according to the Poisson distribution.^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

## Platform Technical Comparison

### Physical Specifications

| Feature | eMAG | MagNA PURE 96 | easyMAG |
|---------|------|---------------|---------|
| **Dimensions (W×D×H)** | 142×80×181 cm | 136×81.5×100 cm | 100×65×53 cm |
| **Extraction Time** | ~90 min | ~90 min | ~60 min |
| **Sample Capacity** | 24+24 (48 total) | 96 | 24 |

### Operational Characteristics

| Feature | eMAG | MagNA PURE 96 | easyMAG |
|---------|------|---------------|---------|
| **Sample Loading** | Direct from original tube (1.5-14 mL) | Robotic loader required to working plate | Manual loading to working cartridge |
| **Barcode Reader** | Built-in (simplifies operation) | Manual barcode reader | Manual barcode reader |
| **Nucleic Acid Collection** | Direct to collection tubes | To collection plate, then robotic transfer to PCR plate/tubes | Collected in cartridge, manual transfer to tubes |
| **User Convenience** | Complex (2 independently operated 24-sample subunits) | Very easy (linear 16-sample racks) | Very easy (manual cartridge loading) |
| **Lab Size Fit** | Medium-sized | Large-sized | Small-sized |

### Operational Considerations

#### eMAG Operational Challenges

- **Without barcoded tubes:** Complex and cumbersome sample loading
- **With barcoded tubes:** Simple load-and-run working mode
- **Dual subunit design:** 2 independently operated 24-sample units
  - **Disadvantage:** Confusing when loading full 48 samples without barcodes
  - **Advantage:** Flexible for ≤24 sample runs
- **Requires:** Well-trained technician for proper operation

#### MagNA PURE 96 Advantages

- **User-friendly:** Very easy to operate
- **High throughput:** 96 samples per run
- **Linear loading:** Samples loaded in 16-sample racks
- **Cons:** Requires additional robotic machine for loading, extra transfer steps

#### easyMAG Advantages

- **Fastest:** ~60 min extraction time
- **Established:** Well-documented performance, extensive validation
- **Simple:** Easy to operate
- **Cons:** Limited capacity (24 samples), semi-automated

## Recommendations

### For High-Volume Laboratories (>50 samples/run)

**Recommendation:** MagNA PURE 96

**Rationale:**
- Maximum throughput (96 samples)
- User-friendly operation
- Suitable for laboratories processing large volumes of respiratory samples annually

### For Medium-Sized Laboratories (25-50 samples/run)

**Recommendation:** eMAG

**Rationale:**
- Balanced automation (48 samples)
- Space-efficient
- Optimal with barcoded sample tubes
- Good compromise between easyMAG's speed and MagNA PURE 96's capacity

### For Low-Volume Laboratories (<25 samples/run)

**Recommendation:** easyMAG

**Rationale:**
- Fastest turnaround time (~60 min)
- Established reliability
- Easy to operate
- Smallest footprint
- Proven performance in clinical settings

### Sample Type Considerations

All platforms demonstrated >97% agreement across:
- Bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL)
- Tracheal aspirations
- Nasopharyngeal swabs

**Note:** Viscous samples (BAL, tracheal aspirations) required dilution with M199 transport media for all platforms.

## Conclusions

### Primary Conclusion

All three extraction platforms demonstrated comparable analytical and clinical performance for detection of common respiratory viruses. The choice of platform should be based on:

1. **Laboratory volume** (samples per run)
2. **Workflow requirements** (automation level, sample loading preferences)
3. **Operational constraints** (space, technician training, barcode infrastructure)

### Validation Summary

| Criterion | Status | Details |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Analytical Sensitivity** | ✅ Pass | >98% across all platforms |
| **Clinical Specificity** | ✅ Pass | 97.8-100% across all platforms |
| **LOD Performance** | ✅ Pass | Comparable across platforms (differences within one dilution factor) |
| **Inter-platform Agreement** | ✅ Pass | Kappa >0.973 |
| **Sample Type Consistency** | ✅ Pass | >97% agreement across all sample types |

### Clinical Impact

- **High-throughput capacity** without compromising analytical quality
- **Automation options** for various laboratory scales
- **Comparable performance** enables flexibility in platform selection
- **All platforms meet clinical validation criteria** with sensitivity and specificity >97%
