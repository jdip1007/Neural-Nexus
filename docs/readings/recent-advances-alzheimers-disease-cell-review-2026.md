# Recent advances in Alzheimer's disease: From molecular mechanisms to therapeutic strategies

**Source:** Cell, Leading Edge Review (2026)  
**Authors:** Michelle D. Rudman, Jason D. Ulrich, David M. Holtzman  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.06.006  
**Institution:** Washington University, Department of Neurology

## TL;DR

First disease-modifying therapies for Alzheimer's disease (AD) now exist — lecanemab and donanemab slow cognitive decline by ~30% over 18 months through amyloid-β removal. However, cognitive decline persists after amyloid clearance due to co-pathologies (tau, neuroinflammation, lipid dyshomeostasis) that become semi-autonomous. Major advances include: structural elucidation of disease-specific amyloid and tau filaments via cryo-EM, discovery of APOE protective variants, recognition of adaptive immune system contributions, and emergence of plasma biomarkers (p-tau217, p-tau181, GFAP) enabling earlier diagnosis.

## Key Points

### Pathological Mechanisms

#### Amyloid-β (Aβ)
- **Production:** Cleavage of APP by β-secretase (BACE1) and γ-secretase, regulated by synaptic activity and SORL1^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Timeline:** Aβ accumulation begins ~20 years before cognitive impairment^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Structural heterogeneity:** Cryo-EM revealed disease-specific Aβ42 filaments distinct between sporadic and familial AD, and different from synthetically derived fibrils^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Pathogenic species:** Evidence supports Aβ protofibrils (lecanemab) and mature plaques containing pE3-Aβ (donanemab) as most pathogenic; antibodies targeting monomers failed^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

#### Tau
- **Hyperphosphorylation:** Impairs microtubule binding, leads to misfolding, aggregation into filaments and neurofibrillary tangles^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Structural specificity:** Cryo-EM structures of AD tau filaments identical to PART but distinct from CTE, Pick's, CBD, PSP^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Post-translational modifications:** >95 distinct modifications across 88 residues; specific patterns correlate with disease stage and patient heterogeneity^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Propagation:** Spreads trans-synaptically from entorhinal cortex → hippocampus → neocortex; involves cell-autonomous and non-cell-autonomous mechanisms (glial cells, extracellular vesicles)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Therapeutic Advances

#### Anti-Amyloid Antibodies
- **Lecanemab (CLARITY-AD):** 30% slowing of cognitive decline over 18 months; targets Aβ protofibrils; Fc-mediated microglial activation required^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Donanemab (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2):** Similar clinical benefit; targets N-terminal pyroglutamate Aβ (pE3-Aβ)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Mechanism:** Rapid amyloid removal slows tau pathology progression and attenuates neuroinflammation (dose-dependent effects)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Limitations:** Modest slowing relative to amyloid clearance; co-pathologies persist after amyloid removal; earlier intervention shows greater benefit^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

#### Other Therapeutic Approaches
- **Anti-tau antibodies:** Multiple candidates in trials (semorinemab, gosuranemab, etalanetug); mixed results so far^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Targeted protein degradation:** PROTACs and AUTOTACs show efficacy in reducing tau accumulation; tau-targeting RING fusion proteins promote selective degradation of aggregated tau^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Gene therapy:** AAVrh.10 delivery of APOE2-Christchurch variant suppresses amyloid and tau pathology in mice^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **CAR-based therapies:** CAR macrophages, CAR-T cells, and CAR astrocytes targeting amyloid in preclinical development^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### APOE Biology
- **Rare protective variants:** Discovery of APOE variants associated with reduced AD risk has led to breakthroughs in understanding APOE's role in modulating AD risk^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Therapeutic targeting:** APOE2-Christchurch variant gene therapy shows promise in suppressing amyloid and tau pathology^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Neuroimmunology
- **Innate immune system:** Microglial activation and phagocytosis required for anti-amyloid antibody efficacy; TREM2 signaling implicated^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Adaptive immune system:** Recognition of its importance in neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration in AD; immunomodulatory approaches under investigation^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Biomarkers:** GFAP, TSPO (microglia), sTREM2 reflect neuroinflammatory activity along AD continuum^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Biomarker Discovery
- **Fluid biomarkers:** Plasma p-tau217 and p-tau181 track earliest amyloid deposition; p-tau205 correlates with later stages (brain atrophy, hypometabolism)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Plasma brain-derived tau:** Novel biomarker for AD-type neurodegeneration^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Neuroinflammation markers:** Plasma GFAP, sTREM2; PET imaging with TSPO, MAO-B tracers^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Early detection:** Plasma biomarkers enable identification of pathology in cognitively normal adults at risk^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Concepts
- [amyloid-beta](concepts/amyloid-beta.md): Primary constituent of neuritic plaques; Aβ42 more aggregation-prone; protofibrils and pE3-Aβ are pathogenic^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- [tau-pathology](concepts/tau-pathology.md): Hyperphosphorylated tau aggregates into NFT; structural heterogeneity across tauopathies; correlates with cognitive decline^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- [neuroinflammation](concepts/neuroinflammation.md): Microglial and astrocyte activation; TSPO, GFAP, sTREM2 as biomarkers; both innate and adaptive immune involvement^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

## Takeaways

1. **Paradigm shift:** 2023 marked first clear evidence that removing amyloid from brain can slow AD progression, validating amyloid hypothesis after decades of failures^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

2. **Structural biology breakthroughs:** Cryo-EM revealed disease-specific amyloid and tau filament structures, explaining clinical heterogeneity and enabling more targeted therapeutics^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

3. **Early intervention critical:** Earlier treatment (before extensive co-pathology develops) yields greater benefit, highlighting need for better early detection via plasma biomarkers^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

4. **Multi-target approach needed:** Amyloid removal alone insufficient; need complementary therapies targeting tau, neuroinflammation, and lipid dyshomeostasis^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

5. **APOE insights pivotal:** Discovery of protective APOE variants transformed understanding of AD risk and opened new therapeutic avenues^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

6. **Biomarker revolution:** Plasma biomarkers (p-tau217, GFAP, sTREM2) enable accessible early detection and disease monitoring^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

7. **Immune system recognition:** Both innate and adaptive immunity contribute to AD pathogenesis, expanding therapeutic possibilities beyond amyloid and tau^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

8. **Hope for prevention:** Preclinical trials (AHEAD 3-45, TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3) testing prevention in asymptomatic at-risk individuals^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
