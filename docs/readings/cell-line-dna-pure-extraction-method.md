The extraction method follows a systematic approach starting with cell lysis and ending with DNA purification. Key steps include:

1. Cell harvesting and washing
2. Complete cell lysis using specialized buffers
3. Protein removal through precipitation
4. DNA purification and concentration
5. Quality assessment

The method specifies precise buffer formulations including:
- Lysis buffer with appropriate detergents
