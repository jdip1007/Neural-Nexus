# It's Not Inflation You Should Be Watching. It's The Boomers.

**Source:** The Infographics Show, YouTube. Published June 22, 2026. 256,884 views ^[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D1KomlztSuA]

## TL;DR

While inflation is cooling and unemployment is low, the real economic story is **Baby Boomers who control nearly $79 trillion in wealth** and collect hundreds of billions in annual interest income from higher rates. The video argues that policies meant to slow spending are often enriching people who already own the most assets, creating a "Reverse Robin Hood Heist" where generational wealth transfer is reshaping the economy more than traditional inflation concerns.

## Core Thesis

The real story isn't inflation—it's **who owns everything while everyone else tries to catch up**. Decades of asset inflation have created the largest wealth concentration in modern American history, with high interest rates potentially helping the wealthy more than hurting them.
