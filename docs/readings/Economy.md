# Economy

The economy refers to the entire system of production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services in a particular region or country. It encompasses all economic activities and interactions between individuals, businesses, and governments.

## Economic Systems

### Capitalism
- Private ownership of means of production
- Market-driven pricing and allocation
- Profit motive and competition
- Consumer sovereignty
- Limited government intervention

### Socialism
- Collective or state ownership
- Central planning and coordination
- Redistribution of wealth
- Social welfare programs
- Public services and utilities

### Mixed Economies
- Combination of market and planned elements
- Regulation and oversight
- Social safety nets
- Private enterprise with public oversight
- Balancing efficiency and equity

## Economic Sectors

### Primary Sector
- Agriculture and farming
- Mining and extraction
- Forestry and fishing
- Natural resource management
- Raw material production

### Secondary Sector
- Manufacturing and production
- Construction and building
- Processing and refining
- Industrial operations
- Value addition activities

### Tertiary Sector
- Services and retail
- Healthcare and education
- Finance and banking
- Transportation and logistics
- Entertainment and hospitality

### Quaternary Sector
- Information technology
- Research and development
- Knowledge management
- Innovation and creativity
- Digital services

## Economic Indicators

### Macroeconomic Indicators
- GDP (Gross Domestic Product)
- Inflation rates
- Unemployment rates
- Interest rates
- Exchange rates

### Microeconomic Indicators
- Supply and demand
- Consumer prices
- Producer costs
- Market competition
- Business profits

## Economic Cycles

### Business Cycles
- Expansion phases
- Peak periods
- Contraction phases
- Trough periods
- Recovery phases

### Market Cycles
- Bull markets
- Bear markets
- Sideways markets
- Volatility periods
- Trend reversals
