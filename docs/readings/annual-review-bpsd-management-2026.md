# Management of Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia: Nonpharmacologic and Pharmacologic Interventions

**Source:** Watt JA, Ismail Z, Lane N, Seitz D, Goodarzi Z. Annual Review of Clinical Psychology. 2026;22:397-425. DOI: 10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-061724-085912

## TL;DR

**97% of people with dementia experience at least one BPSD within 5 years of diagnosis.** This comprehensive review demonstrates that nonpharmacologic interventions (psychological, behavioral, sensory, environmental) show comparable or superior efficacy to pharmacologic treatments with significantly fewer harms. The authors advocate for a **person-centered care model** that prioritizes understanding the meaning behind behaviors, addressing unmet needs, and implementing tiered approaches (nonpharmacologic first, pharmacologic only when necessary). Despite growing evidence, clinical uptake of nonpharmacologic interventions remains problematic while psychotropic overuse persists.

## Study Design

- **Type:** Systematic review and synthesis
- **Scope:** Nonpharmacologic and pharmacologic interventions for BPSD across all dementia types and stages
- **Evidence Base:** RCTs, network meta-analyses, observational studies, clinical guidelines
- **Framework:** Person-centered dementia care model + DICE algorithm (Describe, Investigate, Create, Evaluate)
- **Symptom Domains Covered:** Agitation, psychosis, depression, anxiety, apathy, sleep disturbances, inappropriate sexual behaviors, eating disturbances

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Finding | Implication |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| BPSD Prevalence | 97% within 5 years of diagnosis ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] | Universal concern, not edge cases |
| Antipsychotic NNH (mortality) | 26 (1 extra death per 26 treated) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] | Significant harm that cannot be ignored |
| Nonpharmacologic efficacy | Similar or greater than pharmacologic for agitation/depression ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] | Should be first-line, not fallback |
| Psychotropic overuse | Persistent in clinical settings despite guidelines ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] | Implementation gap between evidence and practice |

## Core Framework: 6-Step Integration Model

### 1. Search for Meaning in Behavior
Behaviors are not random—they reflect unmet needs, feelings, or responses to environment. Contributing factors include:
- **Unmet needs:** Food, affection, meaningful activity ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Pain:** Undiagnosed/undertreated pain manifests as agitation/aggression ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Social stressors:** Communication difficulties with caregivers ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Environmental factors:** Overstimulation/understimulation, temperature, lighting ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Lifelong patterns:** Night shift workers may continue inverted sleep schedules ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### 2. Understand the Person
- Lifelong behavioral patterns provide context for current behavior ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Personality traits influence symptom expression ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Previous coping strategies may still work ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### 3. Identify Protective and Predisposing Factors

**Protective Factors:**
- Familiar caregiver presence ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Familiar environment ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Caregiver dementia knowledge ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Support availability for caregivers ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Sensory aids (glasses, hearing aids) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Tailored dementia care plan ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Predisposing Factors:**
- Over/understimulating environment ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Vision/hearing impairment ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Comorbid psychiatric diagnoses ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Worsening dementia severity ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Caregiver burden/distress ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### 4. Identify Precipitating and Perpetuating Factors

**Precipitating Factors:**
- Pain, hunger, thirst ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Medication changes ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Temperature discomfort ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Sleep disturbances ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Perpetuating Factors:**
- Poor communication strategies ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Inadequate treatment of precipitating factors ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Poor care plan implementation ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Lack of caregiver support ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### 5. Establish Clinically Meaningful Change
**Measurement tools:**
- **Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI):** 12 domains, frequency/severity/caregiver distress ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Symptom-specific scales:** CMAI (agitation), CSDD (depression), AES (apathy) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Goal attainment scaling:** Personalized goals, person-centered outcomes ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### 6. Prioritize Nonpharmacologic Interventions
**Four categories:**
1. **Psychological:** Reminiscence therapy, CBT, psychoeducation ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
2. **Behavioral:** Activity modification, structured routines, caregiver education ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
3. **Sensory:** Music therapy, aromatherapy, massage, bright light therapy ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
4. **Environmental:** Outdoor activities, environmental modifications, reducing overstimulation ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Symptom-Specific Approaches

### Agitation
**Nonpharmacologic (First-line):**
- Music therapy (strong evidence) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Caregiver education ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Structured activities ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Environmental modifications (reduce overstimulation) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Pharmacologic (Consider only if risk of harm):**
- Atypical antipsychotics (limited efficacy, high risk) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Antidepressants (mixed evidence) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### Depression
**Nonpharmacologic:**
- Psychotherapy ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- CBT ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Exercise ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Reminiscence therapy ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Pharmacologic:**
- SSRIs (modest benefits, small effect sizes) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### Sleep Disturbances
**Nonpharmacologic:**
- Sleep hygiene education ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Bright light therapy ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Physical activity ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Environmental modifications ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Pharmacologic:**
- Melatonin (limited evidence) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Trazodone (modest efficacy, adverse effects) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### Apathy
**Nonpharmacologic:**
- Structured activities ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Exercise ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Caregiver education (engagement strategies) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Behavioral activation ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Pharmacologic:**
- Methylphenidate (modest efficacy in RCTs) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## DICE Algorithm

**D - Describe:** Contextualize and characterize the concerning behavior ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
**I - Investigate:** Identify patient, caregiver, and environmental contributing factors ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
**C - Create:** Collaborative care plan with tiered, nonpharmacologic interventions first ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
**E - Evaluate:** Assess effectiveness, adjust as needed ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Person-Centered Language Paradigm

**Shift from medical terminology:**
- "Behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia" → "Responsive behaviors" ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- "Patient is agitated" → "Person is calling out requesting food" ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- "Aggressive" → Describing specific behavior (e.g., pacing, restless) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Rationale:**
- People with dementia are responding to needs/feelings/environment ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Promotes development of nonpharmacologic interventions ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Aligns with person-centered dementia care model ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- More inclusive for people with dementia and caregivers ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Challenge:**
- Measurement requires valid/reliable outcome measures ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Need mapping between medical research terminology and point-of-care language ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Deprescribing Framework

**Process:**
1. Identify medications no longer beneficial or where harms outweigh benefits ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
2. Gradual tapering to avoid withdrawal symptoms ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
3. Monitor for recurrence of BPSD ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
4. Implement nonpharmacologic interventions to support deprescribing ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Evidence:**
- Successful deprescribing demonstrated in multiple settings ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Reductions in antipsychotic use without worsening BPSD ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Takeaways

1. **Nonpharmacologic interventions are first-line:** They have comparable or superior efficacy to pharmacologic treatments with significantly fewer harms ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

2. **Behaviors have meaning:** Understanding unmet needs, pain, environmental factors, and lifelong patterns is essential for effective management ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

3. **Person-centered care is the foundation:** Care plans should be co-created by multidisciplinary teams, caregivers, and people with dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

4. **Antipsychotics carry significant risks:** Number needed to harm for mortality is 26—benefits must be carefully weighed ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

5. **Implementation gap persists:** Despite growing evidence, clinical uptake of nonpharmacologic interventions remains problematic while psychotropic overuse continues ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

6. **Use the DICE algorithm:** Describe, Investigate, Create, Evaluate provides a structured approach to BPSD management ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

7. **Deprescribing is feasible:** Antipsychotics and other psychotropic medications can be successfully deprescribed with proper support ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

8. **Health equity matters:** Social determinants significantly influence BPSD experience and access to care ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
