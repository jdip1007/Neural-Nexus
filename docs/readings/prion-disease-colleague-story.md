## Core Topics Covered

### Prion Disease
- Prion diseases are neurodegenerative disorders caused by misfolded proteins
- Characterized by progressive cognitive decline and memory loss
- Always fatal, with no known cure
- The video documents watching a colleague forget who people are^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

### Historical Prion Diseases
Based on the extensive references provided:

1. **Kuru** (New Guinea):
   - Endemic degenerative CNS disease in Eastern Highlands
   - First described in NEJM papers documenting clinical, pathological, and epidemiological features^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]
   - Connected to cannibalistic practices
   - Journey from Papua New Guinea to Neanderthals' extinction^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

2. **Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease (CJD)**:
   - First described in 1920 by Creutzfeldt and Jakob
   - Peculiar focal disease of the central nervous system^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]
   - Sporadic CJD MRI documentation included^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

3. **Scrapie**:
   - Prion disease in sheep
   - Studies on heat stability and chromatographic behavior^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]
   - Connection to kuru documented^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

4. **Bovine Spongiform Encephalopathy (BSE)**:
   - "Mad Cow Disease" in cattle
   - First description as novel progressive spongiform encephalopathy^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

5. **Yeast Prions**:
   - URE3 as altered URE2 protein in Saccharomyces cerevisiae^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]
   - Evidence for prion analogs in simple organisms

## Key Characteristics of Prion Diseases
Based on the cited literature:

1. **Protein-Only Infectivity**: Prions are misfolded proteins that can propagate their conformation without nucleic acids^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

2. **Heat Stability**: Studies show prions survive conditions that destroy conventional pathogens^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]

3. **Spongiform Encephalopathy**: Brain tissue develops characteristic sponge-like holes

4. **Cross-Species Transmission**: Documented from sheep to humans (kuru) and cattle to humans (BSE)^[raw/videos/prion-disease-colleague-story.md]
