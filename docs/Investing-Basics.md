# Investing Basics

Investing is the process of allocating resources, usually money, with the expectation of generating an income or profit. It involves purchasing assets that are expected to provide returns over time.

## Core Investment Principles

### Risk Management
Understanding and managing risk is fundamental to successful investing:
- **Risk tolerance**: Assess your ability to withstand potential losses
- **Risk diversification**: Spread investments across different asset classes
- **Risk-reward balance**: Higher potential returns typically involve higher risk

### Asset Allocation
The strategy of dividing your investment portfolio among different asset categories:
- **Stocks**: Ownership shares in companies
- **Bonds**: Fixed-income securities representing loans
- **Real estate**: Physical property and REITs
- **Commodities**: Raw materials and agricultural products
- **Cash equivalents**: Highly liquid assets

### Time Horizon
Investment time frames significantly impact strategy:
- **Short-term**: Less than 3 years (focus on preservation)
- **Medium-term**: 3-10 years (balanced growth and stability)
- **Long-term**: 10+ years (growth-oriented strategies)

## Investment Vehicles

### Traditional Investments
- **Stocks**: Direct ownership in companies
- **Bonds**: Debt instruments issued by governments or corporations
- **Mutual funds**: Pooled investment vehicles managed by professionals
- **ETFs**: Exchange-traded funds that track indexes or sectors

### Alternative Investments
- **Real estate**: Physical property and REITs
- **Private equity**: Investments in private companies
- **Hedge funds**: Alternative investment strategies
- **Cryptocurrency**: Digital assets and blockchain-based tokens

### Retirement Accounts
- **401(k)**: Employer-sponsored retirement plans
- **IRA**: Individual retirement accounts
- **Roth IRA**: Tax-advantaged retirement accounts

## Investment Strategies

### Passive Investing
- **Index funds**: Track market indexes
- **Buy-and-hold**: Long-term investment approach
- **Dollar-cost averaging**: Regular investing regardless of market conditions

### Active Investing
- **Value investing**: Focus on undervalued companies
- **Growth investing**: Focus on companies with high growth potential
- **Momentum investing**: Follow market trends and patterns

## Key Investment Metrics

### Performance Indicators
- **Return on Investment (ROI)**: Measure of investment profitability
- **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**: Yearly interest rate
- **Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)**: Year-over-year growth rate

### Risk Metrics
- **Standard deviation**: Measure of volatility
- **Beta**: Measure of relative risk compared to market
- **Sharpe ratio**: Risk-adjusted return measure

## Common Investment Mistakes

### Emotional Decision Making
- **Fear and greed**: Emotional responses to market movements
- **Herding behavior**: Following market trends without analysis
- **Overconfidence**: Underestimating investment risks

### Poor Planning
- **No clear goals**: Investing without specific objectives
- **Insufficient diversification**: Concentrated portfolios
- **Ignoring fees**: High costs eroding returns
