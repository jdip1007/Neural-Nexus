# Insurance Comparison

Comparison of major insurance product types and their characteristics.

## What's Compared

Savings insurance vs. term life insurance + separate investment, based on analysis from the Bowtie Insurance video exposing savings insurance myths.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]

## Comparison Matrix

| Factor | Savings Insurance | Term Life + Investment |
|--------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Transparency** | Low | High |
| **Flexibility** | Low | High |
| **Control** | Limited | Full |
| **Costs** | High | Low |
| **Liquidity** | Poor | Excellent |
| **Expected returns** | Below projections | Market returns |
