# Peter Sheridan Dodds

**Peter Sheridan Dodds** is a network scientist and complex systems researcher at the University of Vermont. He has contributed to the understanding of scaling laws in complex networks.

## Key Contributions

- Research on scaling in complex networks
- Work on sentiment analysis and social networks
- Contributions to understanding universal scaling patterns
- Collaborated with researchers at the Santa Fe Institute
