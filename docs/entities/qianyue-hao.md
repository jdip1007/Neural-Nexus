# Qianyue Hao

**Role**: Researcher, Beijing National Research Center for Information Science and Technology (BNRist), Tsinghua University

**Affiliation**: Tsinghua University, Beijing, P.R. China

## Overview

Qianyue Hao is a researcher in electronic engineering at Tsinghua University's BNRist. Hao co-authored the landmark 2026 *Nature* paper analyzing AI's impact on science, which revealed the paradox of individual gains versus collective narrowing in AI-augmented research.

## Key Facts

- **Institution**: Tsinghua University, Department of Electronic Engineering
- **Research Center**: Beijing National Research Center for Information Science and Technology (BNRist)
- **Notable Work**: "Artificial intelligence tools expand scientists' impact but contract science's focus" (Nature, 2026)
- **Collaborators**: Fengli Xu, Yong Li, James Evans
