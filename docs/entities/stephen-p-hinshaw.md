# Stephen P. Hinshaw

## Overview

Stephen P. Hinshaw is a prominent psychologist and researcher specializing in ADHD, developmental psychopathology, and mental health stigma. He holds dual appointments at the University of California, Berkeley (Department of Psychology) and the University of California, San Francisco (Department of Psychiatry).^[raw/articles/hinshaw-2018-adhd-review.md]

## Key Facts

- **Affiliations:** UC Berkeley (Psychology) and UCSF (Psychiatry)
- **Email:** hinshaw@berkeley.edu
- **Research focus:** ADHD, developmental psychopathology, externalizing spectrum disorders, mental health stigma, girls with ADHD
- **Landmark work:** Berkeley Girls ADHD Longitudinal Study — prospective follow-up of girls with ADHD from childhood into adulthood
- **Key publications:** *The ADHD Explosion* (with Scheffler, 2014); Annual Review chapter on ADHD (2018)

## Research Contributions

### ADHD in Girls and Women

Hinshaw pioneered the prospective study of girls with ADHD, challenging the male-centric view of the disorder. His Berkeley Girls ADHD Longitudinal Study followed 140+ girls with ADHD and comparison girls from childhood through early adulthood, revealing:^[raw/articles/hinshaw-2018-adhd-review.md]
- Continuing cross-domain impairment into adolescence and adulthood
- Elevated risk for self-injury, suicide attempts, and internalizing disorders
- Distinct patterns of comorbidity (internalizing vs. externalizing)
- Parenting stress as a mediator of outcomes

### Developmental Psychopathology Framework

Hinshaw advocates for a developmental psychopathology perspective that integrates multiple levels of analysis — from genes to cultural forces — rejecting purely biological or purely social explanations.^[raw/articles/hinshaw-2018-adhd-review.md]

### Stigma and Mental Health

His work on mental health stigma (with Martinez 2016; with Stier 2008) examines how stigma affects recognition, treatment-seeking, and policy for ADHD and other conditions.^[raw/articles/hinshaw-2018-adhd-review.md]
