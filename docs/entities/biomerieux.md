# bioMérieux

## Overview

French in vitro diagnostics company specializing in nucleic acid extraction platforms and clinical laboratory automation. Manufacturer of the easyMAG and eMAG extraction systems.

---

## Key Facts

**Location:** France^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Industry:** In vitro diagnostics, laboratory automation

---

## Products

### easyMAG

- **Type:** Semi-automated nucleic acid extraction platform
- **Capacity:** 24 samples
- **Technology:** Silica-based (BOOM technology)
- **Launch:** 2005 (second-generation platform)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Applications:** Total nucleic acid extraction from biological samples^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Extraction time:** ~60 minutes^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### eMAG

- **Type:** Fully automated nucleic acid extraction platform
- **Capacity:** 24+24 samples (48 total)
- **Technology:** Silica-based (BOOM technology)
- **Features:**
  - Direct extraction from primary tubes (1.5-14 mL)
  - Built-in barcode reader (enables simple workflow with barcoded tubes)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
  - Dual independently operated 24-sample subunits^[raw/laborary/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
  - Direct transfer to PCR strips or storage tubes^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Extraction time:** ~90 minutes^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Target laboratories:** Medium-sized laboratories^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

## Technology

### BOOM Technology

- **Full name:** Boom, Sol, Salimans, and Schut method^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Principle:** Silica particles bind DNA and RNA in high salt concentrations, elute in low-salt buffers
- **Reference:** Boom R, Sol CJ, Salimans MM, Jansen CL, Wertheim-van Dillen PM, van der Noordaa J (1990). Rapid and simple method for purification of nucleic acids. J Clin Microbiol 28: 495-503.^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Status:** Gold standard for universal extraction of RNA and DNA^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

## Performance Validation

### easyMAG Performance

- **Extensively validated** for clinical sample nucleic acid extraction^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Applications:**
  - Hepatitis C virus and HIV nucleic acid amplification testing^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
  - RNA extraction from stool suspensions with MS-2 coliphage control^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
  - Various clinical specimen types^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### eMAG Performance

- **Comparable performance** to easyMAG in clinical sample evaluation^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Clinical sensitivity:** >99% vs easyMAG^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Clinical specificity:** >98% vs easyMAG^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Kappa coefficient:** >0.973 (excellent agreement with comparison platforms)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
