# Camiel M. van der Laan

## Overview

Camil M. van der Laan is a geneticist and researcher specializing in ADHD genetics and genome-wide association studies. As lead author of the landmark 2025 Nature Genetics meta-analysis, van der Laan has made significant contributions to understanding the genetic architecture of ADHD through large-scale collaborative research.

## Key Facts

- **Role**: Lead author on the largest ADHD genetics meta-analysis to date
- **Contribution**: Coordinated international collaboration across 28 cohorts and 124 researchers
- **Publication**: "Genome-wide association meta-analysis of childhood ADHD symptoms and diagnosis identifies new loci and potential effector genes" (Nature Genetics, 2025)
- **Impact**: Discovery of 39 independent loci (17 new) and 8 novel ADHD effector genes
- **Methodological Innovation**: Combined ADHD symptoms and diagnosis data to improve genetic discovery power

## Research Contributions

### ADHD Genetics Research
Van der Laan's 2025 meta-analysis represents a significant advancement in ADHD genetics:

#### Major Findings
- **39 independent loci** identified through genome-wide association meta-analysis
- **17 novel loci** discovered, expanding the known genetic landscape of ADHD
- **8 novel effector genes** with high confidence (EMCN, STK32C, PCDH17, TCF12, PEAK1, IGF1R, CTNNA2, ABCA12)
- **Continuum model validation**: Strong evidence that ADHD represents the extreme end of a continuous genetic liability distribution

#### Methodological Innovations
- **Combined approach**: Integration of ADHD symptom measures with clinical diagnosis data
- **Large-scale collaboration**: Coordinated 28 cohorts with 70,953 unique individuals
- **Advanced statistical methods**: Utilization of FLAMES analysis for causal gene identification
- **Comprehensive pathway analysis**: Integration of synaptic and cell adhesion pathway insights

### Scientific Leadership
- **International collaboration**: Successfully coordinated complex multi-cohort research project
- **Data integration**: Combined diverse ADHD assessment instruments and raters
- **Quality control**: Implemented rigorous statistical methods to ensure robust findings
- **Open science**: Contributed to understanding of ADHD genetic architecture through publication in high-impact journal
