# Orna Mor

## Overview

Clinical virologist and researcher at the Central Virology Laboratory, Ministry of Health, Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel. Expert in respiratory virus diagnostics and molecular testing methodologies.

- Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel
