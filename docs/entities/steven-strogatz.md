# Steven Strogatz

**Steven Strogatz** is a mathematician at Cornell University known for his work on complex systems, synchronization, and network theory. He is a prominent science communicator and author.

## Key Contributions

- Research on synchronization in complex networks
- Work on small-world networks with Duncan Watts
- Contributions to understanding universal scaling patterns
- Author of "Sync" and "The Joy of x"
- Prominent science communicator
