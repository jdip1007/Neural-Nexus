# Musa Hindiyeh

## Overview

Clinical virologist and researcher at the Central Virology Laboratory, Ministry of Health, Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel. Expert in respiratory virus detection and nucleic acid extraction platform validation.

---

## Key Facts

**Affiliation:**
- Central Virology Laboratory, Ministry of Health
- Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel
- Department of Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine, School of Public Health, Sackler Faculty of Medicine, Tel-Aviv University, Tel-Aviv, Israel^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Role:**
- Co-author (equal contribution) on eMAG vs MagNA PURE 96 vs easyMAG validation study
- Contributed to study conceptualization, validation, and writing^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

---

## Research Contributions

**Method Validation:**
- Evaluation of eMAG, MagNA PURE 96, and easyMAG extraction platforms for respiratory virus detection^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- Assessment of analytical sensitivity, specificity, and clinical performance across 262 samples^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Virus Expertise:**
- Influenza A (including H1N1pdm) and B
- Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)
- Human Metapneumovirus (hMPV)
- Parainfluenza-3
- Adenovirus^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
