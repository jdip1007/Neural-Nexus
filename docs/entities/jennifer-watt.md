## Research Focus

Jennifer Watt's research centers on:
- **Behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD):** Comparative efficacy of interventions for aggressive and agitated behaviors in dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Deprescribing in vulnerable populations:** Development and validation of STOPPCog criteria for deprescribing in cognitively vulnerable older people ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Pharmacologic safety:** Safety of pharmacologic interventions for neuropsychiatric symptoms in dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Comparative effectiveness research:** Network meta-analyses comparing nonpharmacologic and pharmacologic interventions for BPSD ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Depression in dementia:** Comparative efficacy of interventions for reducing symptoms of depression in people with dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Antipsychotic harms:** Comparative risk of harm associated with trazodone or atypical antipsychotic use in older adults with dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Guideline development:** Systematic review of guideline recommendations on behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Virtual care:** Barriers and facilitators to virtual care in geriatric medicine clinics ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
