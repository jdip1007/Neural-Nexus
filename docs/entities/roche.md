# Roche

## Overview

German pharmaceutical and diagnostics company specializing in nucleic acid extraction platforms and clinical laboratory automation. Manufacturer of the MagNA PURE 96 extraction system.

---

## Key Facts

**Location:** Germany^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Industry:** In vitro diagnostics, laboratory automation, pharmaceuticals

---

## Products

### MagNA PURE 96

- **Type:** Fully automated nucleic acid extraction platform
- **Capacity:** 96 samples^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Technology:** Magnetic bead technology^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Launch:** 2009^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Applications:** Clinical sample nucleic acid extraction, high-throughput workflows^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Extraction time:** ~90 minutes^[raw/laborary/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

## Performance Validation

### Clinical Performance

- **Excellent nucleic acid extraction efficiency** for clinical samples^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Cytomegalovirus testing:** Validated for CMV nucleic acid amplification testing in clinical samples^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### Comparative Performance (vs eMAG)

- **Overall sensitivity:** 97.7%^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Overall specificity:** 99.5%^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **PPV:** 98.3%^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **NPV:** 99.3%^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Kappa coefficient:** >0.973 (excellent agreement with eMAG)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### Limit of Detection

| Virus | LOD (MagNA PURE 96) | Comparison |
|-------|---------------------|------------|
| Influenza A | 10⁻³ | Better than easyMAG (10⁻²), equal to eMAG (10⁻³) |
| Influenza B | 10⁻³ | Equal to easyMAG and eMAG |
| H1N1pdm | 10⁻⁴ | Equal to easyMAG and eMAG |
| Adenovirus | 10⁻⁵ | Equal to easyMAG and eMAG |
| RSV | 10⁻⁵ | Worse than easyMAG (10⁻⁶), equal to eMAG |
| Parainfluenza-3 | 10⁻⁵ | Equal to easyMAG and eMAG |
| hMPV | 10⁻³ | Equal to easyMAG, worse than eMAG (10⁻⁴) |^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Note:** LOD differences within one dilution factor are considered clinically negligible.

## Operational Characteristics

### Advantages

- **Very easy to operate:** User-friendly design^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **High throughput:** 96 samples per run suitable for high-volume laboratories^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Linear sample loading:** Samples loaded in 16-sample racks^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### Disadvantages

- **Requires additional robotic machine** for sample loading^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Extra transfer steps:** Nucleic acids collected in plate, then robotic transfer to PCR plates or collection tubes^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- **Manual barcode reader** (no built-in system)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

---

## Target Laboratories

**Optimal for:** Large-sized laboratories with:
- High sample volume (>50 samples/run)
- Robotic infrastructure available
- User-friendly workflow requirements
- 96-well plate-based workflows^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
