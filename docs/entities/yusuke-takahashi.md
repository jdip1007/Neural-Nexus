# Yusuke Takahashi

## Overview

Yusuke Takahashi is a researcher at the Super-network Brain Physiology laboratory, Graduate School of Life Sciences, Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan. He is the first author of the 2026 Communications Biology paper on the [rem-sleep-energy-paradox](concepts/rem-sleep-energy-paradox.md).

## Key Facts

- **Position**: Researcher, Super-network Brain Physiology, Graduate School of Life Sciences, Tohoku University
- **Funding**: Data Science Program in Tohoku University
- **Key contribution**: First author on "Energy paradox in REM sleep: balancing supply and consumption in brain metabolism" (Communications Biology, 2026)
- **Research focus**: Wide-field fluorescence imaging of brain energy dynamics, sleep neurophysiology
