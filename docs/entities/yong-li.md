## Overview

Yong Li is a researcher at Tsinghua University's BNRist and Zhongguancun Academy. Li is corresponding author of the landmark 2026 *Nature* paper analyzing AI's impact on science, which revealed the paradox of individual gains versus collective narrowing in AI-augmented research.
