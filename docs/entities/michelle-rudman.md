# Michelle D. Rudman

## Overview

Michelle D. Rudman is a neurology researcher at Washington University specializing in Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis, biomarkers, and therapeutic strategies. Co-author of comprehensive reviews on recent advances in AD research with [[david-holtzman]] and [[jason-ulrich]].^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

## Key Facts

### Institution and Roles
- **Affiliation:** Washington University in St. Louis, Department of Neurology^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- **Centers:** Hope Center for Neurological Disorders, Knight Alzheimer's Disease Research Center^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Research Focus
- Alzheimer's disease pathological mechanisms
- Biomarker discovery and validation
- Disease-modifying therapies^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

## Research Contributions

### Major Publications
