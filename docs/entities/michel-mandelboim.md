# Michal Mandelboim

## Overview

Clinical virologist and researcher at the Central Virology Laboratory, Ministry of Health, Chaim Sheba Medical Center, Ramat-Gan, Israel. Expert in respiratory virus diagnostics, molecular testing methodologies, and nucleic acid extraction platform validation.

## Research Contributions

### Method Validation Studies

**Lead Research:**
- Comparison of eMAG, MagNA PURE 96, and easyMAG extraction platforms for respiratory virus detection (262 samples, 6 viruses)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- Evaluation of Simplexa Flu A/B & RSV for direct detection of influenza viruses and RSV^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

**Key Findings:**
- All three extraction platforms demonstrated comparable performance (>97% sensitivity, >98% specificity)
- Inter-platform agreement: Kappa >0.973^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- LOD differences within one dilution factor (clinically negligible)^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]

### Laboratory Scale

**Annual Sample Volume:**
- >10,000 respiratory samples per year processed at Central Virology Laboratory^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
- Majority of samples require nucleic acid extraction for molecular diagnostics^[raw/laboratory/validation/emag-magana-easymag-respiratory-virus-extraction-comparison.md]
