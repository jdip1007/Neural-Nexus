# Tohoku University

## Overview

Tohoku University (東北大学) is a national university located in Sendai, Japan. It is one of Japan's Imperial Universities and a leading research institution, particularly in neuroscience and life sciences.

## Key Facts

- **Location**: Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, Japan
- **Type**: National university (Imperial University)
- **Relevant labs**: Super-network Brain Physiology (Graduate School of Life Sciences and Graduate School of Medicine)
- **Notable for**: Brain energy dynamics research, glial biology, wide-field fluorescence imaging
