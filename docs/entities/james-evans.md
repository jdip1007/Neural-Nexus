## Overview

James Evans is a professor at the University of Chicago and external professor at the Santa Fe Institute. Evans is corresponding author of the landmark 2026 *Nature* paper analyzing AI's impact on science, which revealed the paradox of individual gains versus collective narrowing in AI-augmented research.^[raw/articles/ai-impacts-science-nature-2026.md]

## Background

James Evans directs the Knowledge Lab at the University of Chicago, studying how knowledge is produced, consumed, and transformed in science and society. His work spans computational social science, bibliometrics, and the sociology of innovation. The Santa Fe Institute affiliation reflects expertise in complex systems and network science.
