# David M. Holtzman

## Overview

David M. Holtzman is a prominent neurology researcher and Alzheimer's disease expert at Washington University School of Medicine. He is the senior author of major reviews on recent advances in AD research, including groundbreaking work on [amyloid-beta](concepts/amyloid-beta.md), [tau-pathology](concepts/tau-pathology.md), [neuroinflammation](concepts/neuroinflammation.md), and .^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

### Key Research Areas

#### Biomarker Discovery
- Plasma biomarkers for early AD detection (p-tau217, p-tau181, GFAP)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- Fluid biomarkers for disease staging and monitoring
- Relationship between biomarkers and clinical progression^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

#### Disease-Modifying Therapies
- Analysis of anti-amyloid antibody trials (lecanemab, donanemab)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- Mechanisms of therapeutic benefit and limitations
- Future directions for AD therapeutics^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]

#### Neuroimmunology
- Role of innate and adaptive immune systems in AD^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- Microglial and astrocyte contributions to pathogenesis
- Therapeutic targeting of neuroinflammation

#### APOE Biology
- Rare protective APOE variants and their implications^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
- APOE as therapeutic target
- Gene therapy approaches (AAVrh.10-APOE2-Christchurch)^[raw/articles/recent-advances-alzheimers-disease-cell-review-2026.md]
