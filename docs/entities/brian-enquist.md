# Brian Enquist

## Overview

**Brian Enquist** is an ecologist and evolutionary biologist known for co-developing the **WBE Theory** (West-Brown-Enquist theory) of biological scaling. His work provided key insights into how fractal distribution networks in organisms explain universal scaling laws like Kleiber's Law. He contributed to the Veritasium video on metabolic scaling by explaining WBE theory's origins and assumptions.^[raw/videos/one-billion-heartbeats-veritasium.md]

## Key Facts

- **Field:** Ecology, evolutionary biology, theoretical biology
- **Key Contribution:** Co-developer of WBE theory with West and Brown
- **Expertise:** Fractal networks, scaling laws, ecological patterns
- **Significance:** Helped develop theoretical explanation for biological scaling

## Research Focus

### Ecological Scaling
How scaling laws apply to:
- **Organismal physiology:** Metabolism, growth, lifespan
- **Population dynamics:** Abundance, range size, dispersal
- **Community structure:** Species diversity, interaction networks
- **Ecosystem processes:** Energy flow, nutrient cycling

### Theoretical Biology
Developing mathematical models for:
- **Fractal networks** in organisms
- **Allometric relationships** across species
- **Universal patterns** in biological systems
- **Evolutionary constraints** on organismal design
