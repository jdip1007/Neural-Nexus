# Yoko Ikoma

## Overview

Yoko Ikoma is a researcher at the Super-network Brain Physiology laboratory, Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan. She is a co-author of the 2026 Communications Biology paper on the [rem-sleep-energy-paradox](concepts/rem-sleep-energy-paradox.md) and contributed to the development of the experimental method and tools.

## Key Facts

- **Position**: Researcher, Super-network Brain Physiology, Tohoku University
- **Funding**: Grant-in-Aid for Early-Career Scientists (22K15218, 24K18234), Grant-in-Aid for Scientific Research (C) (26K09537)
- **Key contributions**: 
  - Co-author on the REM sleep energy paradox paper (2026)
  - Development of experimental methods and tools for wide-field fluorescence imaging
  - Co-author on "Local brain environment changes associated with epileptogenesis" (Brain, 2022)
- **Research focus**: Fluorescence imaging methods, brain environment monitoring
