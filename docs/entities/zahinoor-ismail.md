## Research Focus

Zahinoor Ismail's research centers on:
- **Neuropsychiatric symptoms:** Behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD) ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Dementia subtypes:** Alzheimer's disease, dementia with Lewy bodies ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Psychotic symptoms in dementia:** Cholinesterase inhibitors for treatment of psychotic symptoms ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Apathy:** Apathy in neurocognitive disorders, functional neuroimaging of appetite loss ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Dementia diagnosis:** Recommendations of the Canadian Consensus Conference on the diagnosis and treatment of dementia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Psychopharmacology:** Aripiprazole treatment for psychotic episodes in schizophrenia ^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
