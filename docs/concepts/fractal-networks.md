# Fractal Biological Networks

**Fractal networks** are self-similar branching structures found in biological distribution systems (blood vessels, capillaries, plant vascular systems). The fractal-like properties of these networks are central to the WBE theory of metabolic scaling.

## Key Properties

- **Self-similarity**: Network structure repeats at different scales
- **Area-preserving branching**: Daughter vessels maintain parent area
- **Terminal unit invariance**: Capillary size is independent of body size
- **Space-filling**: Networks reach all parts of the organism

## Role in WBE Theory

The fractal network model explains why metabolic rate scales as M^(3/4) rather than M^(2/3) (surface law). The 3/4 exponent arises from the geometry of fractal-like distribution networks that minimize energy dissipation.
