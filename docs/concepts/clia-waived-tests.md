# CLIA Waived Tests

**CLIA waived tests** are simple laboratory tests with low risk of error that are exempt from most CLIA regulatory requirements. They are cleared by the FDA for home use or classified as simple enough to warrant waiver status.

## Characteristics

- Simple methodology with minimal steps
- Low risk of erroneous results
- No routine proficiency testing required
- No personnel training requirements beyond manufacturer instructions
- Certificate of Waiver sufficient to perform

## Examples

- Glucose meter testing
- Urine pregnancy tests
- Rapid strep tests
- Home-use test kits
