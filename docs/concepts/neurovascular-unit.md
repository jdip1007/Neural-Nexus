# Neurovascular unit

The [[neurovascular-unit]] (NVU) is a conceptual and functional framework describing the integrated system of neurons, glial cells, and blood vessels that work together to maintain brain homeostasis. It represents a paradigm shift from neuron-centric models of brain function toward a multicellular understanding of how the brain operates as a coordinated ecosystem.

## Components

### Neurons
- Primary signaling units of the brain
- Generate electrical and chemical signals
- Communicate with vasculature to regulate blood flow via [[neurovascular-coupling]]

### Glial Cells

**Astrocytes:**
- Form endfeet around blood vessels, regulating [[blood-brain-barrier]] function
- Modulate synaptic activity and neurotransmitter clearance
- Participate in [[glymphatic]] waste clearance
- Provide metabolic support to neurons

**Microglia:**
- Resident immune cells of the CNS
- Monitor brain health and respond to injury/infection
- Contribute to synaptic pruning during development
- Become activated in response to metabolic overload and inflammation

**Oligodendrocytes:**
- Produce [[myelin]] that insulates axons
- Support adaptive myelination based on neuronal activity
- Provide metabolic support to axons

### Vasculature
- Endothelial cells with tight junctions forming the BBB
- Pericytes regulating capillary diameter and blood flow
- Smooth muscle cells in larger vessels
- Basement membrane providing structural support

## Functions

### Neurovascular Coupling (NVC)
The process by which active neurons signal to nearby blood vessels to increase local cerebral blood flow, ensuring adequate delivery of oxygen and glucose to support metabolic demands.

### Blood-Brain Barrier Maintenance
Astrocyte endfeet and pericytes regulate the selective permeability of the BBB, controlling passage of substances between blood and brain tissue.

### Metabolic Support
The NVU coordinates delivery of nutrients (glucose, oxygen) and removal of waste products (CO2, metabolic byproducts) to maintain neuronal function.

### Waste Clearance
The [[glymphatic]] system, dependent on astrocyte-mediated CSF flow, clears protein aggregates and metabolic waste from brain tissue during sleep.

### Adaptive Myelination
Neuronal activity patterns influence oligodendrocyte maturation and myelin formation, optimizing neural signal transmission efficiency.

## Vulnerability to Metabolic Overload

The NVU is highly sensitive to metabolic disturbances:

1. **Visceral adiposity** creates systemic inflammation that damages BBB integrity
2. **Insulin resistance** impairs neuronal energy metabolism and vascular signaling
3. **Chronic inflammation** activates microglia and astrocytes, disrupting normal communication
4. **Vascular dysfunction** reduces blood flow and impairs NVC
5. **Impaired clearance** leads to accumulation of toxic protein aggregates

## Clinical Significance

### Neurodegenerative Diseases
NVU dysfunction is implicated in:
- [[Alzheimer-disease]]: BBB breakdown, reduced glymphatic clearance
- [[Parkinson-disease]]: Vascular pathology, microglial activation
- [[vascular-dementia]]: Impaired NVC, chronic cerebral hypoperfusion

### Metabolic Disorders
Obesity and type 2 diabetes directly impact NVU function:
- Systemic inflammation compromises BBB
- Insulin resistance affects neuronal and vascular signaling
- Impaired glymphatic function during sleep disturbances

### Therapeutic Targets
The NVU offers multiple intervention points:
- Vascular protection (BBB integrity, NVC enhancement)
- Anti-inflammatory approaches (microglial modulation)
- Glymphatic enhancement (sleep optimization)
- Metabolic normalization (weight loss, insulin sensitization)

## Research Advances

### Single-Cell Omics
Revealing cell-type-specific responses to metabolic stress within the NVU.

### Spatial Transcriptomics
Mapping gene expression patterns across different NVU components in tissue context.

### Intravital Imaging
Real-time observation of NVU function in living organisms.

### Genetic Targeting
Manipulating specific cell populations to understand their contributions to NVU dysfunction.

- Disruption at any component can propagate through the entire system
- Regional differences in NVU resilience create selective vulnerabilities
- The NVU is the central pathway linking obesity to neurodegeneration
