# Investment Returns

**Investment returns** measure the gain or loss on an investment over time, expressed as a percentage of the initial investment.

## In Insurance Context

Savings insurance "expected returns" are sales projections, not guarantees. Only 6 out of 135 analyzed policies met projected returns.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
