# Technology

Technology refers to the application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes, especially in industry. It encompasses tools, systems, methods, and processes used to solve problems and improve human life.

## Key Aspects

- **Innovation**: The development of new ideas, methods, and tools
- **Engineering**: The application of scientific and mathematical principles
- **Automation**: The use of technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention
- **Digital Transformation**: The integration of digital technology into all areas of business
- **Cybersecurity**: Protection of digital systems and data from threats
