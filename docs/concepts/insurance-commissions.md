# Insurance Commissions

**Insurance commissions** are sales incentives paid to insurance agents, typically front-loaded in the first few years of a policy. High commission structures on savings insurance create conflicts of interest, as agents may recommend products that benefit them financially rather than the customer.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]

## Impact

- Agents may not disclose underperformance statistics
- Sales pressure toward high-commission products
- Consumer recommendations may be biased
