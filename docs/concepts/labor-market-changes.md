# Labor Market Changes

## Definition

Labor market changes refer to the evolving dynamics of work, employment, compensation, and worker rights in response to technological advancement, globalization, demographic shifts, and economic policy changes. These changes fundamentally alter the relationship between labor and capital, affecting wealth distribution, economic opportunity, and social mobility across generations.

## Current Labor Market Context

### "Death of Labor" Phenomenon
- **"Death of Labor":** Economic changes reducing labor's share of economic gains ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Wage-productivity gap** growing as workers receive smaller share of output
- **Labor market changes** affecting economic opportunity ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Wealth accumulation** increasingly through asset ownership rather than labor ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Generational Economic Shifts
- **Baby Boomer generation** controlling unprecedented wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Younger generations** facing different economic conditions and challenges
- **Intergenerational inequality** through wealth concentration ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Economic opportunity** increasingly tied to family wealth rather than individual effort ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

## Key Labor Market Dynamics

### 1. Wage Stagnation
- **Real wage growth** failing to keep pace with productivity increases
- **Income inequality** widening between high-skill and low-skill workers
- **Middle-class erosion** as traditional employment opportunities decline
- **Wealth concentration** through asset appreciation rather than wage growth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### 2. Employment Structure Changes
- **Gig economy growth** reducing traditional employment protections
- **Automation displacement** of routine and manual labor
- **Remote work transformation** changing workplace dynamics
- **Skills gap** between worker capabilities and employer requirements

### 3. Worker Bargaining Power
- **Union decline** reducing collective bargaining strength
- **Non-compete agreements** limiting worker mobility
- **At-will employment** creating job insecurity
- **Gig worker classification** denying traditional labor protections

### 4. Benefits and Security
- **Healthcare costs** shifting to workers and families
- **Retirement insecurity** as traditional pensions disappear
- **Income volatility** increasing for non-salaried workers
- **Work-life balance** challenges in modern economy
