# Inheritance Trap

## Definition

The "Inheritance Trap" describes how intergenerational wealth transfer through inheritance creates systemic barriers to economic opportunity for those without inherited advantages. This phenomenon concentrates wealth among descendants of affluent families while limiting upward mobility, effectively bypassing merit-based economic systems and perpetuating inequality across generations.

### Wealth Transfer Mechanism

- **Economic Opportunity Bypass:** Inherited advantages becoming more important than individual effort ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Merit System Erosion:** Traditional economic systems undermined by inherited wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Concentration Effects
