# CMS — Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services

The **Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS)** is the US federal agency that administers CLIA and enforces laboratory regulations under 42 CFR Part 493.

## Role in Laboratory Regulation

- Enforces CLIA regulations
- Certifies laboratories (waiver, compliance, accreditation)
- Conducts or oversees laboratory inspections
- Approves proficiency testing programs
- Issues sanctions for non-compliance
