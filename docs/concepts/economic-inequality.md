# Economic Inequality

## Definition

Economic inequality refers to the unequal distribution of economic resources, including income, wealth, and opportunities, among individuals or groups within a society. It encompasses both income inequality (unequal distribution of earnings) and wealth inequality (unequal distribution of assets and property).

## Economic Mechanisms

### 1. Asset Inflation
- Decades of asset price growth creating wealth concentration ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Real estate, stocks, and other assets appreciating over generations ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Housing markets increasingly favoring those with inherited advantages ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### 2. Interest Rate Policy
- **"Luxury Paradox":** High interest rates create luxury for asset owners while causing hardship for borrowers ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Policy Misalignment:** Economic policies designed to control inflation may enrich the wealthy ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Wealth Transfer Mechanism:** Interest income becoming primary wealth accumulation ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### 3. Housing Market Dynamics
- **"Cash Buyer Cartel":** Wealthy buyers purchasing homes without mortgage financing ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Generational Competition:** Younger generations competing against inherited wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Property Value Inflation:** Demand from wealthy buyers driving up prices ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### 4. Labor Market Changes
- **"Death of Labor":** Economic changes reducing labor's share of economic gains ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Wage-Productivity Gap:** Growing disconnect between worker output and compensation
- **Job Market Polarization:** High-skill, high-wage vs. low-skill, low-wage opportunities
- **Automation Impact:** Technology displacing traditional employment
