# Risk Assessment

**Risk assessment** in the laboratory is the systematic process of identifying hazards, analyzing risks, and implementing mitigation measures to protect personnel and ensure safe operations.

## Risk Assessment Process

1. **Hazard identification** — List all potential hazards (biological, chemical, physical, ergonomic)
2. **Risk analysis** — Assess likelihood and severity of each hazard
3. **Risk evaluation** — Compare to acceptable risk criteria, prioritize
4. **Risk mitigation** — Implement controls (engineering → administrative → PPE)
5. **Monitoring and review** — At least annually, or when procedures change

## Risk Categories

| Category | Examples | Mitigation |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Biological | Bloodborne pathogens, aerosols | BSL-2, BSC, sharps disposal |
| Chemical | Hazardous reagents, toxins | Fume hood, PPE, SDS |
| Physical | Electrical, mechanical | GFCI, guarding, training |
| Ergonomic | Repetitive motion, lifting | Workstation design, rotation |
