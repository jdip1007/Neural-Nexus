# Investment Vehicles

**Investment vehicles** are financial instruments that allow individuals to invest capital for growth. Common types include index funds, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, and stocks.

## Common Types

- **Index funds**: Low-cost, broad market exposure (e.g., S&P 500)
- **ETFs**: Exchange-traded funds, tradeable like stocks
- **Mutual funds**: Actively managed portfolios
- **Bonds**: Fixed-income securities
- **Stocks**: Direct equity ownership

## Why It Matters

When separating insurance from investment (as recommended over savings insurance), choosing appropriate investment vehicles is critical for long-term wealth building.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
