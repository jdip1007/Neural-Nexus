# Opportunity Cost

**Opportunity cost** is the potential benefit lost when choosing one investment over another. For savings insurance, the opportunity cost is the difference between locked-up returns and what could be earned through alternative investments.

## In Insurance Context

Capital locked in savings insurance for decades cannot be invested elsewhere. The opportunity cost is often significant given that ~95% of policies underperform projections.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
