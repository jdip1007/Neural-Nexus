# Culture

Culture encompasses the social behavior, institutions, and norms found in human societies, as well as the knowledge, beliefs, arts, laws, customs, capabilities, and habits of individuals in these groups. Culture is a complex whole that includes knowledge, belief, art, morals, law, custom, and any other capabilities and habits acquired by humans as members of society.

## Dimensions of Culture

- **Material Culture**: Physical objects, resources, and spaces that people use to define their culture
- **Non-material Culture**: Ideas, beliefs, values, and norms that shape behavior
- **High Culture**: Cultural activities or products that are often associated with intellectual or artistic achievements
- **Popular Culture**: Cultural activities or products that are consumed by the general population
- **Digital Culture**: Cultural practices and norms emerging from digital technologies and internet usage

## Cultural Impact of Technology

The digital age has transformed cultural expression, communication, and social interaction. Social media, streaming platforms, and online communities have created new forms of cultural production and consumption, influencing how identities are formed and maintained.

## Cultural Studies

Cultural studies examines how culture creates and transforms individual experiences, everyday life, and social relations. It explores the production and consumption of cultural artifacts within their social and historical contexts.
