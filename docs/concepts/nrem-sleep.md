# NREM Sleep

## Definition

Non-rapid eye movement (NREM) sleep is the predominant sleep stage, characterized by delta-frequency oscillations in the EEG/ECoG alongside other frequency bands including theta. During NREM, the brain exhibits stable energy dynamics with tight coupling between neuronal activity and blood flow.

## Key Characteristics

- **ECoG**: Dominant delta-frequency oscillations, with theta-band (6–9 Hz) activity also present at lower power
- **EMG**: Moderate muscle tone
- **Blood flow**: BBV fluctuations are relatively localized, with fast anterior-to-posterior vascular waves
- **Metabolism**: Stable neuronal ATP levels; astrocytic pyruvate and neuronal ATP are balanced
- **Neurovascular coupling**: Theta-band activity predicts subsequent BBV changes with ~4–5 s delay

## NREM-Specific Vascular Dynamics

- Theta-band (6–9 Hz) ECoG power closely predicts BBV fluctuations
- The FIR-BRF (finite-impulse-response brain-blood-volume response function) captures this coupling
- Vascular waves propagate from anterior to posterior cortex
- Suggests a homeostatic feedforward mechanism for energy delivery
