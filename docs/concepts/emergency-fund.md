# Emergency Fund

An **emergency fund** is a liquid savings reserve, typically 3-6 months of living expenses, kept in an accessible high-yield savings account to cover unexpected costs without disrupting long-term investments.

## Why It Matters

An emergency fund provides financial resilience without the lock-up penalties and low returns of savings insurance.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
