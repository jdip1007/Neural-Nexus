# Luxury Paradox

## Definition

The "Luxury Paradox" describes how high interest rates intended to control inflation and help ordinary people actually create luxury conditions for existing asset owners while causing hardship for borrowers. This phenomenon represents a fundamental contradiction in monetary policy where interventions meant to help the majority often benefit the wealthy minority through generational wealth mechanisms.

## Core Concept

### Policy Contradiction
- **Intended Purpose:** High interest rates designed to slow spending and control inflation ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Actual Effect:** Creates luxury conditions for asset owners through massive interest income ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Borrower Impact:** High rates cause financial hardship for those with mortgages and loans ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Wealth Transfer Mechanism
- **Interest Income Generation:** Hundreds of billions in annual interest income benefiting existing wealth holders ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Asset Value Protection:** High rates help maintain asset values for existing owners ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Wealth Concentration:** Monetary policy accelerates wealth concentration through interest mechanisms ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
