# Demographic Wall

## Definition

The "Demographic Wall" describes the significant economic and social consequences that will occur as Baby Boomers transfer their massive accumulated wealth to younger generations. This represents a fundamental economic transformation point where demographic shifts will reshape markets, policies, and economic opportunity structures across society.

### Wealth Transfer Consequence
