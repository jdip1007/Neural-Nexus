# Demographic Economic Impact

## Definition

Demographic economic impact refers to how changes in population structure, age distribution, and generational cohorts affect economic outcomes, wealth distribution, and market dynamics. These demographic shifts create both opportunities and challenges for economic systems, wealth management, and policy development across different generations.
