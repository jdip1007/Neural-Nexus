# Laboratory Director

The **Laboratory Director** is the qualified individual responsible for overall laboratory operations, ensuring compliance with CLIA and accreditation requirements.

## CLIA Requirements

- Must be an MD, DO, or PhD with specific qualifications
- Responsible for method verification and validation approval
- Ensures quality management system implementation
- Oversees personnel training and competency
- Approves laboratory procedures and policies
