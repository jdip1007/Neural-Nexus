# Engineering

Engineering is the application of scientific and mathematical principles to design, build, and maintain structures, machines, systems, and processes. It involves creativity, technical knowledge, and practical problem-solving.

## Key Areas

- **Mechanical Engineering**: Design and construction of mechanical systems
- **Electrical Engineering**: Study and application of electricity and electronics
- **Software Engineering**: Design, development, and maintenance of software systems
- **Civil Engineering**: Design and construction of infrastructure
- **Chemical Engineering**: Application of chemical principles to industrial processes

## Engineering Principles

- **Problem Solving**: Identifying problems and developing effective solutions
- **Design Thinking**: Creating innovative and practical solutions
- **Systems Thinking**: Understanding how components interact within larger systems
- **Technical Excellence**: Maintaining high standards of quality and performance
- **Safety and Reliability**: Ensuring designs are safe and reliable
