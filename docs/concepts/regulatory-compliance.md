# Regulatory Compliance

**Regulatory compliance** in the clinical laboratory encompasses adherence to all applicable federal, state, and accreditation requirements governing laboratory testing.

## Key Regulatory Frameworks

- **CLIA** (42 CFR Part 493) — Federal laboratory standards
- **CAP** — College of American Pathologists accreditation
- **ISO 15189** — International laboratory quality standard
- **FDA** — 21 CFR 809 (IVD device regulations)
- **State regulations** — State-specific requirements (NY, CA, etc.)

## Compliance Components

- Personnel qualifications and competency
- Method verification and validation
- Quality control programs
- Proficiency testing participation
- Documentation and record keeping
- Inspections and corrective actions
