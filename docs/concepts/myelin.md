# Myelin

Myelin is a multilamellar lipid-rich membrane that wraps around axons, providing electrical insulation and dramatically increasing the speed of action potential propagation. It is produced by [[oligodendrocyte]] cells in the central nervous system (CNS) and Schwann cells in the peripheral nervous system (PNS). Myelin is a critical component of the [[neurovascular-unit]], and its formation and maintenance are regulated by [[adaptive-myelination]] processes.

## Structure

### Myelin Sheath Architecture

**Multilamellar structure:**
- Composed of multiple concentric layers of glial cell membrane
- Each layer wraps around the axon, creating a compact, insulated structure
- Approximately 80% lipid, 20% protein (unique composition compared to most biological membranes)

**Key components:**
- **Myelin basic protein (MBP)**: Maintains compaction of myelin layers
- **Proteolipid protein (PLP)**: Major structural protein in CNS myelin
- **Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG)**: Surface protein involved in immune interactions
- **Cholesterol**: Major lipid component, essential for membrane structure

### Nodes of Ranvier

**Gaps in myelin sheath:**
- Regularly spaced unmyelinated segments (~1 μm long)
- Action potential "jumps" from node to node (saltatory conduction)
- High density of voltage-gated sodium channels
- Essential for rapid, efficient signal propagation

**Internodes:**
- Myelinated segments between nodes
- Length varies with axon diameter
- Thicker myelin → faster conduction velocity

## Function

### Saltatory Conduction

**Speed enhancement:**
- Action potentials jump from node to node
- Reduces membrane capacitance, increases resistance
- Conduction velocity 5-100× faster than unmyelinated axons
- Energy-efficient transmission

**Temporal precision:**
- Enables synchronous firing across neural networks
- Critical for timing-dependent processing
- Supports oscillatory rhythms (gamma, theta)

### Metabolic Support

**Axonal support:**
- Oligodendrocytes provide metabolic substrates to axons
- Lactate shuttle delivers energy to axons
- Myelin maintenance requires continuous metabolic support

**Structural support:**
- Provides mechanical stability to axons
- Protects axons from damage
- Supports axonal transport

## Myelination Processes

### Developmental Myelination

**Timing:**
- Begins prenatally, continues through early adulthood
- Different regions myelinate at different rates
- Sensory/motor pathways myelinate first, association areas last

**Regulation:**
- Activity-dependent mechanisms
- Genetic programming
- Environmental factors (nutrition, stimulation)

### Adaptive Myelination

**Dynamic process:**
- Myelination continues throughout life
- Responsive to neuronal activity patterns
- Optimizes network function and learning

**See:** [[adaptive-myelination]] for detailed mechanism

## Dysfunction in Obesity

### Impairment Mechanisms

1. **Metabolic overload:**
   - Reduced oligodendrocyte metabolic support
   - Impaired myelin lipid synthesis
   - Energy deficits in myelination process

2. **Insulin resistance:**
   - Insulin signaling important for oligodendrocyte function
   - Impaired oligodendrocyte differentiation
   - Reduced myelin protein synthesis

3. **Inflammation:**
   - Cytokines damage oligodendrocytes
   - Microglial activation impairs myelination
   - Chronic inflammation prevents adaptive responses

4. **Vascular dysfunction:**
   - Reduced blood flow limits metabolic support
   - Impaired delivery of myelination precursors
   - Hypoperfusion affects oligodendrocyte health

### Consequences

**Structural changes:**
- Reduced myelin thickness
- Abnormal myelin composition
- Disrupted nodal organization
- White matter hyperintensities

**Functional effects:**
- Slower conduction velocity
- Impaired neural synchrony
- Cognitive processing deficits
- Reduced signal fidelity

## Clinical Significance

### Neurodegenerative Diseases

**Alzheimer's disease:**
- White matter degeneration early in disease
- Myelin breakdown precedes gray matter loss
- Contributes to cognitive decline

**Multiple sclerosis:**
- Autoimmune attack on myelin
- Demyelination and remyelination cycles
- Oligodendrocyte damage

**Vascular dementia:**
- Chronic hypoperfusion damages myelin
- White matter lesions characteristic
- Disrupts connectivity

### Metabolic Disorders

**Obesity:**
- Accelerated white matter aging
- Reduced white matter integrity
- Correlates with cognitive impairment

**Type 2 diabetes:**
- Impaired myelination
- White matter hyperintensities
- Cognitive decline

### Developmental Disorders

**Dysmyelination disorders:**
- Genetic mutations affecting myelin proteins
- Leukodystrophies
- Pelizaeus-Merzbacher disease (PLP mutation)

## Biomarkers

### Imaging

**Diffusion tensor imaging (DTI):**
- Measures white matter tract organization
- Fractional anisotropy (FA) reflects myelin integrity
- Radial diffusivity (RD) sensitive to demyelination

**Myelin water fraction (MWF):**
- Specific to myelin content
- Quantitative measure of myelin water

**Magnetization transfer ratio (MTR):**
- Sensitive to macromolecular content
- Reflects myelin density

### Fluid Markers

**Blood:**
- Myelin basic protein (MBP) fragments
- Neurofilament light chain (NfL)
- Myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG) antibodies

**CSF:**
- Elevated MBP indicates demyelination
- NfL as marker of axonal damage

## Therapeutic Approaches

### Remyelination Promotion

**Pharmacological:**
- Clemastine (antihistamine with remyelination properties)
- Metformin (promotes oligodendrocyte differentiation)
- Experimental remyelinating agents

**Cell therapy:**
- Oligodendrocyte precursor cell transplantation
- Stem cell-derived oligodendrocytes

### Lifestyle Interventions

**Exercise:**
- Aerobic exercise enhances myelination
- Complex skill learning promotes myelin changes
- Physical activity supports white matter health

**Metabolic optimization:**
- Weight loss improves white matter integrity
- Insulin sensitization supports myelination
- Nutritional support for lipid synthesis

## Notes

- Myelin is ~80% lipid, unique among biological membranes
- Saltatory conduction is 5-100× faster than continuous conduction
- Myelination continues throughout life, not just development
- Myelin breakdown is an early event in many neurodegenerative diseases
- Obesity and metabolic disorders accelerate white matter aging
- Remyelination therapies are a major active research area
