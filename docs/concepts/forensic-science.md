# Forensic Science

## Overview

Forensic science is the application of scientific principles and methods to criminal and civil law. It involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of physical evidence in legal proceedings, helping to solve crimes, identify suspects, and exonerate the innocent.

## Core Principles

### Scientific Method in Forensics
- **Evidence Collection**: Systematic collection of physical evidence
- **Chain of Custody**: Maintaining proper chain of custody for evidence
- **Preservation**: Proper preservation of evidence integrity
- **Analysis**: Scientific analysis of evidence
- **Interpretation**: Interpretation of results in legal context

### Objectivity and Impartiality
- **Neutral Analysis**: Objective analysis of evidence without bias
- **Unbiased Interpretation**: Unbiased interpretation of results
- **Ethical Standards**: Maintaining high ethical standards
- **Professional Integrity**: Maintaining professional integrity
- **Transparency**: Transparency in methods and findings
