# Financial Planning

**Financial planning** is the process of setting financial goals and creating strategies to achieve them, including insurance, investments, savings, and retirement planning.

## Core Components

1. **Protection**: Adequate insurance coverage (see [[term-life-insurance]])
2. **Growth**: Investment portfolio (see [[investment-vehicles]])
3. **Liquidity**: Emergency reserves (see [[emergency-fund]])
4. **Risk management**: Understanding [[investment-risk]] and [[opportunity-cost]]

## In Insurance Context

A key principle is separating insurance from investment. Savings insurance combines both at higher cost and lower transparency than purchasing them separately.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
