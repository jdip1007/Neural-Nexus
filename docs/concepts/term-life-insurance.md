# Term Life Insurance

**Term life insurance** (純人壽) is a pure protection insurance product that provides death benefit coverage for a specified period (the "term") without any investment or savings component. It is significantly cheaper than savings insurance because premiums fund only the insurance coverage.

## Key Characteristics

- **No cash value**: Pure protection — no savings or investment component
- **Lower cost**: Typically 10-20x cheaper than equivalent savings insurance
- **Fixed term**: Coverage for a specific period (e.g., 10, 20, 30 years)
- **No surrender value**: If you stop paying, coverage ends — no money back

## Comparison with Savings Insurance

| Factor | Term Life | Savings Insurance |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Cost | Low | High |
| Cash value | None | Accumulates (slowly) |
| Transparency | High | Low |
| Flexibility | High (cancel anytime) | Low (surrender penalties) |

## Why It Matters

Term life insurance is recommended as a cost-effective alternative to savings insurance for pure protection needs. Separating insurance from investment typically yields better outcomes.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
