# Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia (BPSD)

## Definition

Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia (BPSD) are neuropsychiatric symptoms that manifest in all stages and types of dementia. BPSD encompasses a range of symptoms including apathy, agitation, aggression, anxiety, irritability, depression, sleep and nighttime disturbances, aberrant motor behavior, disinhibition, appetite and eating changes, delusions, hallucinations, and euphoria.^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Prevalence

- **97%** of people with dementia experience at least one BPSD within 5 years of diagnosis^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- BPSD occurs across all dementia types and stages^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Prevalence increases with disease progression^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Core Mechanism: Behaviors Have Meaning

BPSD are not random or meaningless. Within the person-centered dementia care model, behaviors are understood as responses to:

**Personal factors (intrinsic to the individual):**
- Unmet needs (food, affection, meaningful activity)^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Pain (often undiagnosed or undertreated)^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Lifelong behavioral patterns (e.g., night shift workers maintaining inverted schedules)^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Personality traits^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Social factors:**
- Communication difficulties with caregivers^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Caregiver burden or distress^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Lack of social support^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Environmental factors:**
- Overstimulation or understimulation^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Suboptimal environmental conditions (too hot/cold, poor lighting, excessive noise)^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Unfamiliar environment^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Vision or hearing impairment^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Anatomical and biochemical changes:**
- Neurodegenerative processes affecting brain circuits^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Neurotransmitter imbalances^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Etiology

The exact causes of BPSD remain incompletely understood, but proposed factors include:^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

- **Personality:** Pre-existing personality traits influence symptom expression^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Genetics:** Genetic factors may predispose to certain BPSD^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Environmental factors:** Overstimulation, understimulation, poor physical environment^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Unmet needs:** Physical, emotional, and social needs not being met^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Anatomical changes:** Neurodegeneration affecting specific brain regions^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Biochemical changes:** Neurotransmitter dysfunction^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Symptom Domains

BPSD is organized into specific domains or symptom clusters:^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

| Domain | Symptoms |
|--------|----------|
| Apathy/Indifference | Lack of motivation, reduced initiative |
| Agitation/Aggression | Restlessness, verbal or physical aggression |
| Depression/Dysphoria | Sadness, hopelessness, tearfulness |
| Anxiety | Worry, tension, restlessness |
| Delusions | False beliefs, paranoid thoughts |
| Hallucinations | Seeing or hearing things that aren't there |
| Disinhibition | Inappropriate social behavior |
| Irritability/Lability | Mood swings, frustration |
| Aberrant Motor Behavior | Pacing, wandering, repetitive movements |
| Nighttime Behavior Disturbances | Sleep-wake cycle disruption |
| Appetite and Eating Changes | Weight loss, food refusal, unusual food preferences |
| Elation/Euphoria | Inappropriately elevated mood |

## Assessment Framework

### DICE Algorithm (Describe, Investigate, Create, Evaluate)

**D - Describe:** Contextualize and characterize the concerning behavior^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- When does it occur?
- How frequently does it occur?
- What does it look like?
- Who is present?

**I - Investigate:** Identify contributing factors^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Patient factors: Pain, hunger, thirst, discomfort, medication changes
- Caregiver factors: Communication style, knowledge, stress
- Environmental factors: Over/understimulation, temperature, lighting, noise

**C - Create:** Collaborative care plan^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Tiered approach: Nonpharmacologic interventions first
- Address contributing factors
- Involve multidisciplinary team, caregivers, and person with dementia

**E - Evaluate:** Assess effectiveness^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Monitor for changes in behavior
- Adjust interventions as needed
- Reassess contributing factors

### Assessment Tools

**Global measures:**
- **Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI):** Assesses 12 neuropsychiatric symptom domains with frequency, severity, and caregiver distress ratings^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Symptom-specific measures:**
- **Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory (CMAI):** Agitation^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Cornell Scale for Depression in Dementia (CSDD):** Depression^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- **Apathy Evaluation Scale (AES):** Apathy^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Person-centered measures:**
- **Goal attainment scaling:** Personalized goals tailored to individual needs^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Impact

**On people with dementia:**
- Greater cognitive decline^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Greater functional decline^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Reduced quality of life^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**On caregivers:**
- Heightened caregiver distress^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Increased burden^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Higher risk of institutionalization^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Mental health impacts (depression, anxiety)^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Management Approaches

### Nonpharmacologic Interventions (First-line)

See [[nonpharmacologic-interventions]] for detailed coverage.

**Four categories:**
1. **Psychological:** Reminiscence therapy, CBT, psychoeducation^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
2. **Behavioral:** Activity modification, structured routines, caregiver education^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
3. **Sensory:** Music therapy, aromatherapy, massage, bright light therapy^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
4. **Environmental:** Outdoor activities, environmental modifications^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Evidence:** Network meta-analyses demonstrate comparable or superior efficacy to pharmacologic interventions with fewer adverse effects^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### Pharmacologic Interventions (Consider only if risk of harm)

**Antipsychotics:**
- Limited efficacy^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Significant harms: Increased mortality (NNH=26), stroke, falls, cognitive decline^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Use only when risk of harm to self or others^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Antidepressants:**
- Modest efficacy for depression^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Small effect sizes^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Risks: Falls, gastrointestinal bleeding, hyponatremia^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Other medications:**
- Benzodiazepines: Falls, confusion, dependence, cognitive decline^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Mood stabilizers: Limited evidence, significant risks^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine: Modest benefits for some symptoms^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

### Deprescribing

See [[deprescribing]] for detailed coverage.

**Process:**
- Gradual tapering^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Monitor for withdrawal symptoms or recurrence^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Implement nonpharmacologic interventions to support deprescribing^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Evidence:** Successful deprescribing demonstrated in multiple settings with reductions in antipsychotic use without worsening BPSD^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Terminology Shift

**Medical terminology:**
- Behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD)
- Neuropsychiatric symptoms

**Person-centered terminology (emerging):**
- Responsive behaviors^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Personal expressions^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Changed behaviors^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Key principle:** Person-centered language emphasizes describing behaviors (e.g., "calling out requesting food") rather than labeling individuals (e.g., "agitated"), which promotes development of nonpharmacologic interventions^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

## Common Misconceptions

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| BPSD are random and meaningless | Behaviors often reflect unmet needs, pain, or responses to environment^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] |
| Antipsychotics are the primary treatment | Nonpharmacologic interventions are first-line with comparable efficacy and fewer harms^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] |
| BPSD are inevitable and untreatable | Multidisciplinary assessments with management plans reduce symptoms^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] |
| One-size-fits-all approaches work | Person-centered, tailored interventions are essential^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] |
| Pharmacologic interventions are always needed | Many BPSD can be managed effectively with nonpharmacologic approaches^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md] |

## Current State

**Evidence base:**
- Growing body of RCTs and network meta-analyses^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Evolution from pharmacologic to nonpharmacologic and person-centered approaches^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Clinical practice:**
- Uptake of nonpharmacologic interventions remains problematic^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Potential overuse of medications persists^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Implementation gap between evidence and practice^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]

**Quality improvement:**
- Quality metrics for antipsychotic use in long-term care^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Educational programs for clinicians and caregivers^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
- Implementation of evidence-based guidelines^[raw/articles/annual-review-bpsd-management-watt-et-al-2026.md]
