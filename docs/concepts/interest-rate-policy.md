# Interest Rate Policy

## Definition

Interest rate policy refers to the decisions made by central banks and monetary authorities regarding the cost of borrowing and the return on savings. This policy tool is used to influence economic activity, control inflation, and stabilize financial markets, but its effects are often unevenly distributed across different segments of the population based on wealth and asset ownership.

## Policy Mechanisms

### 1. Monetary Transmission Channels
- **Interest rate changes** affecting borrowing costs throughout the economy
- **Asset valuation** impacts on stocks, bonds, and real estate
- **Consumer spending** effects through disposable income changes

- **Currency exchange** rates influencing international trade

### 2. Wealth Distribution Effects
- **Asset owners benefit** from higher interest income ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

- **Debtors face increased burden** from existing obligations
