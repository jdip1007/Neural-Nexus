# Wealth Concentration

## Definition

Wealth concentration refers to the disproportionate ownership of assets and financial resources by a small percentage of the population, leading to significant economic inequality. It represents the accumulation of wealth in fewer hands, creating power imbalances and limiting economic opportunity for the majority.
