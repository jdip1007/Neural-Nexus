# Scaling Laws

**Scaling laws** describe how biological variables change with body size according to power-law relationships of the form Y = aM^b, where M is body mass and b is the scaling exponent.

## Key Scaling Exponents

| Relationship | Exponent | Theory |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Metabolic rate vs mass | 3/4 | Kleiber's Law, WBE |
| Surface area vs mass | 2/3 | Surface Law (historical) |
| Heart rate vs mass | -1/4 | WBE prediction |
| Lifespan vs mass | 1/4 | WBE prediction |
| Aortic radius vs mass | 3/8 | WBE prediction |

## Applications

- Predicting physiology from body mass
- Understanding biological network design
- Ecology and biodiversity patterns
- Urban scaling (social interactions scale supralinearly)
