# Insurtech

**Insurtech** (insurance + technology) refers to the use of technology to innovate and improve insurance products, distribution, and customer experience. Digital-first insurers challenge traditional agency models.

## In This Wiki

[[bowtie-insurance]] is an example of a Hong Kong-based insurtech company that uses educational content to challenge traditional insurance sales practices.
