# Cash Buyer Cartel

## Definition

The "Cash Buyer Cartel" describes how wealthy individuals and families, often using generational wealth or accumulated assets, purchase real estate properties without mortgage financing. This creates a competitive disadvantage for traditional buyers who rely on mortgages, driving up property values and limiting access to homeownership for those without inherited advantages.

## Core Concept

### Market Distortion
- **Wealthy Competition:** Cash buyers can outbid mortgage-dependent buyers in competitive markets ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Price Inflation:** Demand from cash buyers drives up property values beyond traditional market fundamentals ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Access Barrier:** Creates significant barriers for first-time buyers and those without inherited wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Generational Wealth Impact
- **Inherited Advantage:** Cash buyers often use wealth transferred through inheritance or family assets ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
