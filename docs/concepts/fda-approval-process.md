# FDA Approval Process

The **FDA approval process** for in vitro diagnostic (IVD) devices determines whether a test method has been cleared or approved by the FDA, which in turn determines whether laboratories perform verification or validation.

## Pathways

- **510(k) Clearance**: Demonstrating substantial equivalence to a predicate device
- **PMA (Premarket Approval)**: For high-risk devices requiring safety and efficacy evidence
- **De Novo**: For novel low-to-moderate risk devices with no predicate

## Impact on Laboratory Testing

- FDA-cleared/approved tests → Laboratory performs **verification**
- Non-FDA tests (LDTs) → Laboratory performs **validation**
- Modified FDA tests → Depends on extent of modification
