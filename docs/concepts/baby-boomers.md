# Baby Boomers

## Definition

Baby Boomers are the generation born between 1946 and 1964, following World War II. This demographic cohort represents one of the largest generational bulges in modern history and currently controls unprecedented levels of wealth and economic influence.

## Demographic Profile

### Generation Timeline
- **Birth Years:** 1946-1964
- **Current Age Range:** 62-80 years old (as of 2026)
- **Peak Influence:** Currently in prime wealth accumulation and retirement years
- **Demographic Bulge:** Unprecedented cohort size affecting economic outcomes ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Current Status
- **Wealth Control:** Approximately $79 trillion in wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- **Economic Influence:** Dominates asset ownership and policy decisions
- **Life Stage:** Transitioning from wealth accumulation to wealth transfer
- **Demographic Impact:** Creating significant economic consequences as they transfer wealth ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

## Economic Impact

### Wealth Concentration
- **Largest wealth concentration in modern American history** ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Control unprecedented levels of financial assets, real estate, and investments
- Benefit from decades of asset inflation and economic growth
- Collect hundreds of billions in annual interest income from higher rates ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Economic Policy Influence
- Shape economic policies through voting power and political influence
- Benefit from policies designed for different economic eras
- Create "Reverse Robin Hood Heist" where policies enrich current asset owners ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- High interest rates help the wealthy more than hurt them ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Housing Market Impact
- **Cash Buyer Cartel:** Enable purchases without mortgage financing ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Drive up property values through demand and investment strategies
- Control significant portions of housing wealth
- Create barriers for younger generations entering the housing market ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

## Generational Wealth Transfer

### Inheritance Patterns
- **"Inheritance Trap":** Wealth transfer limits economic opportunity ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Massive intergenerational wealth transfer occurring as Boomers age
- Creates significant wealth concentration among descendants
- Bypasses merit-based economic systems ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Demographic Economic Consequences
- **"Demographic Wall":** Future economic changes as wealth transfers ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Labor market changes reflect generational shifts and priorities
- Economic structures face significant transformation
- Policy implications for wealth distribution and economic opportunity ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

## Economic Contradictions

### Luxury Paradox
- High interest rates create luxury for asset owners while causing hardship for borrowers ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Economic policies designed to control inflation may enrich the wealthy ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Traditional economic metrics miss generational wealth dynamics ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Policy Misalignment
- Policies designed for different economic eras may not address current realities
- Interest rate policies have different impacts based on wealth ownership ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Economic policies often have opposite intended effects due to wealth concentration ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

## Intergenerational Dynamics

### Economic Inequality
- **"Born too early to explore the universe. Born too late to afford a basic necessity"** ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- Younger generations face different economic conditions and challenges
- Wealth concentration limits upward mobility for non-inherited advantages ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]
- "The real divide isn't just income anymore, it's ownership" ^[readings/infographics-show-boomers-inequality-2026.md]

### Labor Market Impact

- Economic structures shaped by Boomer-era policies and values
