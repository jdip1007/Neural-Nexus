# Automotive

Automotive technology encompasses the design, development, production, and maintenance of vehicles and their systems. It combines mechanical, electrical, and software engineering to create modern transportation solutions.

## Key Areas

- **Vehicle Design**: Aerodynamics, structural engineering, and ergonomics
- **Power Systems**: Internal combustion engines, electric motors, and hybrid systems
- **Electrical Systems**: Wiring, electronics, and control systems
- **Safety Systems**: Airbags, anti-lock brakes, and advanced driver assistance
- **Connected Cars**: Internet connectivity, telematics, and smart features

## Automotive Technology Trends

- **Electric Vehicles (EVs)**: Battery technology, charging infrastructure, and range optimization
- **Autonomous Driving**: Self-driving technology, sensors, and AI systems
- **Connected Vehicles**: IoT integration, remote monitoring, and over-the-air updates
- **Sustainable Materials**: Eco-friendly manufacturing and recyclable components
- **Performance Optimization**: Engine efficiency, emissions reduction, and fuel economy
