# Politics

Politics is the practice and theory of influencing other people on a civic or individual level. More broadly, it refers to achieving and exercising positions of governance — organized control over a human community, particularly a state.

## Key Concepts

- **Power dynamics**: How authority is distributed and exercised
- **Governance systems**: Different forms of political organization
- **Policy making**: The process of creating and implementing rules
- **Political philosophy**: The study of fundamental questions about politics
- **International relations**: How states interact with each other

## Applications

Political analysis helps us understand:
- How decisions are made in societies
- How power structures affect communities
- The relationship between citizens and governments
- Global political trends and conflicts
