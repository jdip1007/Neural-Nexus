# Investment Risk

**Investment risk** is the uncertainty of returns on an investment, including the possibility of losing some or all of the original capital.

## In Insurance Context

Savings insurance is marketed as "low risk" but carries hidden risks: underperformance (95.5% of policies miss projections), liquidity risk (surrender penalties), and opportunity cost.^[raw/videos/savings-insurance-hong-kong-myths-bowtie.md]
