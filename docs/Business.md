# Business

Business refers to the organized efforts of individuals to produce and sell goods and services for profit. It encompasses all activities related to the creation, operation, and management of enterprises that serve customers and generate revenue.

## Business Types

### Sole Proprietorship
- Single ownership and control
- Unlimited liability
- Simple structure and setup
- Direct management and decision-making
- Profits taxed as personal income

### Partnership
- Shared ownership and management
- General and limited partnerships
- Profit and loss sharing
- Joint decision-making
- Legal liability considerations

### Corporation
- Separate legal entity
- Limited liability protection
- Share ownership structure
- Complex governance and reporting
- Double taxation considerations

### LLC (Limited Liability Company)
- Hybrid business structure
- Limited liability protection
- Flexible management options
- Pass-through taxation
- Operational flexibility
